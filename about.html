How we’re built

Somebody holds the whole picture.

Most families end up with a set of specialists who never speak to each other — an attorney here, a CPA there, a broker somewhere else. A Family CFO and the team around them sit in the middle of that group and keep the plan, the documents and the accounts pointing the same way. It is a function, not a person: your family should never depend on any single individual, including us.

Nobody upstream decides what we recommend.

We are independent. No parent company sets a product list, and no proprietary fund has to appear in your portfolio. Where a family needs a specialist we don’t have — an M&A attorney, a forensic CPA — we bring in a vetted one rather than stretch to cover it.

Built to outlast any one person.

Including us, and including you. Everything sits in one place your family can reach, with a written continuity protocol, so nobody is ever handed a shoebox of statements and told to start over.
