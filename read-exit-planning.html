III  ·  Exit Planning

Exit Planning for Business Owners  ·  CEPA

For most owners the business isn’t part of the balance sheet — it is the balance sheet. Decades of work, most of the family’s wealth and much of your identity sit in a single illiquid asset you can convert to cash exactly once.

How we work

Before

Valuation and value-gap analysis, readiness, and the tax and estate work that only counts years out.

During

One seat accountable to your family, alongside your banker, M&A attorney and CPA.

After

A plan for proceeds that now have to last permanently — and the Family CFO relationship that follows.

Who this is for

  • Owners expecting to sell or transition within one to ten years
  • Owners who have had an approach and don’t know what the business is worth
  • Families where the business is most of the net worth, with no plan if something happens first
  • Owners planning an internal transition to family, management or an ESOP

Nearly everything that determines what you keep is decided in the years before a buyer is in the room.
