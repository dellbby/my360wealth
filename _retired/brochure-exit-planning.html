Business Owners  ·  Certified Exit Planning Advisor

For most owners, the business isn’t part of the balance sheet. It is the balance sheet.

Decades of work, almost all of your family’s wealth, and your identity are concentrated in a single illiquid asset — one you cannot diversify, cannot easily borrow against, and can only convert to cash once.

Nearly everything that determines what you walk away with is decided in the years before a buyer is ever in the room.

Your investment banker leaves at closing. Your wealth manager shows up afterward and never knew the business. We’re in all four rooms.
